# Product Overview

## MicroCFO MCP Server

A lightweight Model Context Protocol (MCP) server for AI-powered financial operations with advanced Legal Sentinel capabilities.

### Core Purpose
- **Financial Operations Automation**: AI-powered invoice processing, compliance checking, subsidy discovery, and negotiation assistance
- **Legal Compliance Monitoring**: Real-time monitoring of government legal updates with intelligent relevance filtering
- **Structure-Aware Legal RAG**: Advanced retrieval system that understands legal document structure and preserves context

### Key Features
- **Agent A - Visual Auditor**: Invoice document scanning and structured data extraction
- **Agent B - Legislative Sentinel**: Structure-aware RAG system for legal compliance queries with turnover-based filtering
- **Agent C - Subsidy Hunter**: Government subsidy and scheme discovery
- **Agent D - Negotiator**: Professional email generation for vendor negotiations
- **Real-time Legal Monitoring**: Automated scraping of government websites with WhatsApp alerts

### Target Users
- Small to medium businesses in India
- Chartered Accountants and financial consultants
- Companies requiring GST, Income Tax, and Companies Act compliance
- Businesses with turnover tiers: < 5Cr, 5-20Cr, > 20Cr

### Business Context
- Focuses on Indian legal framework (GST, Income Tax, Companies Act)
- Turnover-based compliance filtering (5 crore, 50 crore thresholds)
- Sector-specific relevance (Textile, Manufacturing, Technology, Trading)
- Conservative CA-style legal interpretations
